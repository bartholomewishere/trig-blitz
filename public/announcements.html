How to read the tables

Group Performance Points come from each group’s placement by average Ranked Game best score (1st +20, 2nd +17, 3rd +14, 4th +11, 5th +8); a student who never played counts as 0. Every successful recitation is worth +0.5. Group Total Points = Group Performance Points + Recitation Points. Classroom behavior points are not included in these tables. Groups tied on points share the same rank and quiz bonus. Only accounts that are still registered are counted.
